#   alias <alias>: <aria-role> [<aria-role> ...]
#       The short alias that sheet lines, @fingerprint items and `name:role` queries use,
#       and the lowercase ARIA roles it stands for (IndexRow.role vocabulary).
#       alias_for(aria_role) is the FIRST alias line listing that role; a role on no line
#       prints as itself. Every role named on an alias line is a known role for `name:role`.

#   prior <aria-role> [<aria-role> ...]: <word>, <word>, <multi word>, ...

#       role is on the left. Words are matched in normal form (lowercase, split on
#       non-alphanumerics, joined by one space), so `sign up` and `sign-up` are the same key.
#       A word may appear on several prior lines; its roles accumulate.
#
#   noun <aria-role> [<aria-role> ...]: <word>, ...      (see the role nouns section)
