Observations, not failures (outside D-408's text; for the spec owner):

1. D-408 (1) is scoped to form context, so a Pay button with no form and no heading shared with a form stays Navigate. I probed this with a temporary file, deleted before commit. A formless "Pay now" with no heading is c# Navigate. A formless "Pay $20.00" under "Order summary" is Navigate. A formless "Complete purchase" or "Confirm" under a "Checkout" heading is Navigate, even when the card fields sit in a frame-1 form (Stripe-style iframe). D-407 raises only a formless "Upgrade" button. A single-page checkout whose card fields are all in a cross-origin iframe, and whose Pay button is in frame 0 outside any form, would run without a Pay ask. This is the same kind of gap as round-3 Should 1. I propose a follow-up decision: Pay words on any button (not a link) are Pay with or without form context, as D-407 already does for "Upgrade".
2. D-408 (2) splits chords at '+', whitespace and control characters, not at '-'. `k#301=Enter-Shift` and `k#301=Ctrl-Enter` are Navigate, while `k#301=Return+Shift` is Pay. If sb-driver accepts '-' as a chord separator, this is a lowering. If sb-driver rejects such keys, it is not. The sb-driver key parser should either reject '-' chords or have sb-act treat '-' as a separator.
3. Carried over from round 3: a bare "Expiry" field (no "card" word) is not card evidence (D-407 Should).
